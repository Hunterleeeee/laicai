# 来财 Branding

## Product name

- Chinese name: `来财`
- Romanized CLI/app identifier: `laicai`

## Logo concept

The logo is designed as a compact wealth mark for a local AI workspace:

- warm gold outer frame for luck, prosperity, and approachability
- deep jade center for calm, grounded tool work
- abstract strokes inspired by `来` and `财`
- upward tilted gold tabs to suggest incoming momentum and good fortune
